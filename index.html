Research Professor · Head of Labour Market & Integration Research

Jacob Nielsen Arendt

I am professor in applied economics at the ROCKWOOL Foundation, with labour economics and migration as the major fields of interests.

I head Labour Market & Integration Research at The ROCKWOOL Foundation in Copenhagen, where I've worked since 2017, following positions as Research Professor at KORA (now part of VIVE) from 2011, and as Associate Professor in Health Economics at the University of Southern Denmark since 2006.

My work combines quasi‑experimental methods — difference‑in‑differences, instrumental variables, regression discontinuity, panel‑data and duration models — with Denmark's population registers to study active labour‑market policy, refugee and immigrant integration, and the economics of education and health. Alongside academic publication, I write regularly for the Danish press and brief ministries, municipalities and international bodies on what the evidence says.
